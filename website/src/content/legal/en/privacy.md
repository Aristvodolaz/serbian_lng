## 2. Key terms

- **Personal data** — any information that relates directly or indirectly to an identified or identifiable individual (the data subject).
- **Processing** — any operation or set of operations with personal data, including collection, recording, organisation, storage, clarification, retrieval, use, transfer, anonymisation, blocking, deletion and destruction.
- **User** — an individual who has registered an account in the App.

## 3. What personal data is processed

The Controller processes only the data that the User provides when registering and using the App, and the data generated during learning:

| Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Registration data | email address, display name (nickname), password hash | provided by the User at registration |
| Learning data | lesson progress, XP (experience points), daily streak, flashcard review history, chosen alphabet (Cyrillic / Latin) | generated automatically during study |
| Technical data | registration date and time, date of last activity | generated automatically |

The Controller **does not collect**: passport data, phone numbers, location, payment details, biometric data, health data, or any special categories of personal data.

Passwords are stored only as an irreversible cryptographic hash (bcrypt). The Controller has no access to the password itself and cannot recover it.

Word pronunciation on flashcards uses the text-to-speech engine built into the device's operating system (Android `TextToSpeech`, iOS `AVSpeechSynthesizer`), on the User's device. The text of the words is not sent anywhere, and no third-party cloud speech service is used.

## 4. Purposes of processing

1. Registration and identification of the User, and access to the account.
2. Providing App features: showing the course, saving and syncing learning progress, awarding XP and streaks, running flashcards and word review.
3. User support — answering requests sent to kap.moral22@gmail.com.
4. Keeping the App secure and preventing fraud and abuse.

Processing for other purposes, including advertising and profiling, **does not take place**.

## 5. Legal grounds

- The consent of the data subject (Article 6 of Law 152-FZ), given at registration in the App (see the separate consent form).
- Performance of a contract (the user agreement), to which the data subject is a party (Article 6(1)(5) of Law 152-FZ).

## 6. Methods and periods of processing

- Processing is carried out by automated means (on the Controller's server) and, where needed for correspondence, by non-automated means.
- Personal data is stored in a database physically located on a server in the Russian Federation, in line with Federal Law No. 242-FZ of 21.07.2014 on the localisation of personal data of Russian citizens.
- Hosting provider: Beget (LLC "Beget"), data centre in the Russian Federation.
- Retention: until the User deletes their account or withdraws consent, unless the law requires otherwise. After account deletion, the data is deleted or anonymised within 30 days.

## 7. Transfer of personal data to third parties

The Controller **does not transfer** personal data to third parties and does not entrust processing to others, except:

- where required by the legislation of the Russian Federation (on a lawful request from a state authority);
- where a hosting provider is needed to store the data (the provider acts as a processor on the Controller's behalf, only to the extent needed to provide hosting services).

Cross-border transfer of personal data **does not take place**. If foreign services are connected in the future (analytics, push notifications, cloud text-to-speech, etc.), this section and section 3 will be updated before any such transfer begins, and the transfer will be notified to Roskomnadzor as required.

## 8. Rights of the data subject

Under Article 14 of Law 152-FZ, the User may:

- obtain information about the processing of their personal data;
- require the data to be corrected, blocked or destroyed if it is incomplete, outdated, inaccurate, unlawfully obtained, or not needed for the stated purpose;
- withdraw consent to processing;
- require the deletion of their account and all related personal data;
- complain about the Controller's actions or inaction to Roskomnadzor or in court.

To exercise these rights, the User writes to kap.moral22@gmail.com. The Controller responds within the time limits set by Article 20 of Law 152-FZ (usually no later than 10 working days; for some requests, 30 days).

## 9. Measures to protect personal data

The Controller takes organisational and technical measures needed to protect personal data from unlawful or accidental access, destruction, alteration, blocking, copying or distribution, including:

- storing passwords only as cryptographic hashes;
- restricting access to the database;
- using a secure connection (HTTPS/TLS) between the App and the server in the production environment.

## 10. Minors

The App sets no minimum age for using the course. Under the rules on personal data: people aged 14 and over may register and consent to the processing of their personal data themselves, to the extent needed to use the App (in line with Article 26 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation on partial legal capacity of minors). For people under 14, registration and the provision of personal data (email address and similar) is done by a parent or guardian on behalf of and in the interest of the child, or with the child's clear consent given at registration to the parent's email address.
